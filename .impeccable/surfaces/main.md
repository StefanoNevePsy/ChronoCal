# ChronoCal main surface
Mode: Operate. Established, user-pinned Taccuino world from DESIGN.md.
Code-first implementation chosen under user's delegated decision. A two-column working sheet places remembered calendar choices beside dates and the resulting report. On narrow screens calendar choices precede the report. No imagery needed. Clear all-day exclusion, overlapping union default, total versus calendar distinction, explicit demo labels. Bound offline OAuth support to local server. Both paper and ink themes, accessible input labels, visible focus, 44px targets.

Scoped extension 2026-10-13: promote Apple Calendar as the no-Google-Cloud source, Internet Accounts setup and explicit native calendar permission; OAuth remains a secondary disclosure. Read accounts as small muted labels beneath names. Preserve the original working sheet and report.
